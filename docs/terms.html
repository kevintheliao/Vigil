What Vigil is

Vigil is a free, on-device app that analyzes your incoming text messages and warns you about possible scams, phishing, and harassment. It is an aid to your own judgment, not a replacement for it.

No guarantee of detection

Vigil uses a machine-learning model, and no model is perfect.

  • Vigil may miss a real scam or threat.
  • Vigil may flag a safe message as suspicious (a false alarm).

Do not rely on Vigil as your only protection. Always be careful with links, payment requests, and messages asking for personal information, even when Vigil shows no warning. If you are in danger or being threatened, contact local emergency services.
